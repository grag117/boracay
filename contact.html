Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Are the package prices final?

No. Prices are educational estimates for this school project, not live quotes. Actual rates, inclusions and availability depend on local providers and travel dates.

Does submitting the form confirm my booking?

No. The form prepares an email inquiry. Your booking is not confirmed until a real provider responds and confirms the arrangements.

What should I bring to the beach?

Consider sun protection, drinking water in a reusable bottle, a towel, suitable footwear and a waterproof pouch. Follow local rules and take all rubbish with you.

Can boat activities be cancelled?

Yes. Sea conditions, weather, safety guidance and local regulations can affect boat trips. Confirm conditions with the licensed operator on the day.

How do I get to Boracay?

Travelers commonly reach the island via Caticlan or Kalibo Airport, then continue by land and boat transfer. Verify current schedules and transfer requirements before travel.
